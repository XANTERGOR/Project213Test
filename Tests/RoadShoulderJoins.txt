Road/junction shoulder coordination (2026-10-10)

Opt-in on Road Junction > mesh foundation (LOD0) > Согласовать обочины дорог.
Default OFF. Existing scene settings and disabled-mode snapshots retain old behavior.
Длина согласования is in metres and capped to Прямой въезд. Applies to connected
Asphalt ends only, not free ends, circular/procedural foundations or intersections
inferred from overlapping meshes. No authored/generated asphalt deformation is added:
width, points, triangles, UV, LOD meshes and socket fit rules stay unchanged.

Road snapshots carry an immutable terrain-only handoff. Shoulder/blend widths and
their cross-section transition from junction to road. Reference terrain heights
compose after the junction with residual alpha, avoiding double-strength flattening.
For paint, residual compensation requires the same active ground layer on both sides;
different layers retain intentional ordered layer compositing, not a colour-match promise.
Grass/stones respect the corresponding removal flags. Displacement suppression and
foundation gizmos follow local widths. Hole/interior foundation geometry stays LOD0.
Selecting an opted-in junction also displays its connected roads' foundation outlines.

Jobs retain their normal paths away from the connected end. The small end region
uses the existing exact-reference height fallback for footprint-dependent weights;
paint composite jobs receive precomputed joined-road masks, as for mesh junctions.
All region keys, bounds and saved detail-placement identity include joining inputs.
Overlapping handoff regions at two junctions are rejected before output publication.

Rebuild the junction, then Apply Pending Changes at LT World if Auto Update is off.
Undo/disable returns to the previous settings through normal rebuild handling.
The feature does not save scenes or migrate source prefabs automatically.
